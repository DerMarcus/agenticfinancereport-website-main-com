Open Mandate · draft v0.1 · 18 September 2026

The mandate, in a form a machine can enforce.

The Agentic Finance Report makes one claim about governance: a boundary that exists only in a prompt is not a boundary. The mandate has to be written down and enforced where the agent cannot reach it. Open Mandate is that mandate as a document: what the agent may do, within what limits, enforced by which layer, revocable by whom, and audited how. It is an open JSON Schema, public domain, and anyone may implement it.

What a mandate contains

The fields come from the report's treasury envelope (chapter 03), its governance tiers (chapter 02) and its five preconditions (chapter 08). Schema: schema.json (JSON Schema 2020-12).

FieldWhat it declares
principalWho answers for the agent: the legal or natural person, its LEI or vLEI, its jurisdiction.
agentName, identity (vLEI, ERC-8004, Solana attestation, Masumi or other) and runtime. holds_keys must be false: the reasoning layer never holds a signing key.
permitted_universeThe instruments the agent may hold or move, by kind and reference.
venues_allowlist · counterparties_allowlistWhere and with whom it may transact. Empty means none.
capsPer transaction, per day, under management, and the time window.
prohibited_actionsWhat is structurally impossible: transfer out, sell, arbitrary calls, change its own mandate.
tiersOperational (the agent acts alone), tactical (it proposes, a person confirms), strategic (people decide without it), and the ticket size above which a person signs.
revocationWho can withdraw the mandate mid-session, by what mechanism, with what timelock.
enforcementFor each limit: the layer (custody, signer, token, rail, identity, runtime), the mechanism, and a URL to the published policy or contract.
auditWhere the ledger is, whether decisions are published before they execute, and in what format.

Conformance

A document that validates against the schema is well-formed. It is conformant when, in addition:

  • every enforcement entry names a control outside the reasoning layer and links to published evidence;
  • agent.runtime.holds_keys is false;
  • prohibited_actions is not empty;
  • revocation names a mechanism that works without the agent's cooperation.

Status vocabulary: declared (submitted by the operator), attested (enforcement independently verified; not yet offered), revoked.

Declare a mandate

  1. Write the document against schema.json. OM-0002 is a complete example for a treasury; OM-0001 for an agent with on-chain custody.
  2. Validate it (any JSON Schema 2020-12 validator) and check the four conformance points above.
  3. Send it to index@agenticfinancereport.com, or open a pull request adding it under mandate/ in the site repository. The Index assigns the ID and lists it as declared. Free, and not an endorsement.
