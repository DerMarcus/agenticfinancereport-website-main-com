Who is responsible

Marcus Maute, Zurich, Switzerland, research@agenticfinancereport.com. This notice applies to agenticfinancereport.com.

What this website does not do

It sets no cookies, identifies no individual visitor, shows no advertising and sells nothing to anybody. There are no forms and no accounts, and the fonts and images are served from this domain rather than from another company.

Measurement

Cloudflare Web Analytics. Since 27 September 2026 the site counts page views using Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is why a small script is loaded from static.cloudflareinsights.com. It sets no cookies, stores no identifier on your device and follows nobody across other websites. What it produces is aggregate: how often a page was viewed, which site a visit came from, the country, the browser and how quickly pages rendered. There is no advertising use, and the figures are not shared with anyone outside the co-authors of this report.

What is processed

Hosting. The website is served by Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc.). To deliver pages and protect the service, Cloudflare processes connection data such as IP addresses, the time of a request and the browser's user agent. Cloudflare may process this data outside Switzerland, including in the United States, under its own data processing terms.

Your choice of view. If you switch between the Human and Agent views, that choice is saved in your own browser (local storage, key afr-mode) so the page opens the same way next time. It never leaves your device, and you can delete it by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Email. If you write to the contact address, your message and address are used only to answer you and are not passed on.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you can ask for information about data concerning you and have it corrected or deleted. Write to the address above.
