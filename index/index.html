Who is actually in production.

A free register of agentic-finance deployments and infrastructure, by layer and status, and of the mandates their agents run under. It grows out of the Agentic Finance Report, whose stack table and five preconditions are its schema. Nobody pays to be listed. Every entry says where its status comes from, and corrections are published.

The three questions

Any institution that transacts with an autonomous agent needs an answer to three things, in a form a machine can check:

  1. Is this agent real, and who stands behind it? Identity: the vLEI, on-chain agent registries and the legal entity they resolve to.
  2. Is it actually bounded, or only instructed? The mandate: permitted universe, caps, tiers, revocation, and the layer that enforces each limit where the agent cannot reach it.
  3. Has it stayed inside its mandate so far? The record: a published, append-only log of what the agent proposed, did and was blocked from doing.

The Index registers the answers operators give to the second question today, and lists the infrastructure that answers the first. The third is the next step: attestation, where an independent party checks that the declared enforcement exists. It is not offered yet, and nothing here is an attestation, a rating or an endorsement.

The rules

  • Nobody pays to be listed. No listing fees, no paid placement, no metered access to the data. The Index is free and its data is public domain (CC0).
  • The listed party does not set its own status. Status comes from a published source, named in each entry. Operators can confirm or dispute an entry; both are recorded.
  • Corrections are public. Every change to an entry is logged below with its date and reason. Entries are never silently edited.
  • The editor's own projects are listed under the same rules and carry a disclosure line. The Index will be operated independently of every organisation it lists; until the separate entity exists, this page states who runs it.

Register a deployment or a mandate

Listing is free. Two ways in:

  • A deployment. Email index@agenticfinancereport.com with the operator, the layer, what runs, the jurisdiction, and a public source for its status (a regulatory filing, a client reference, on-chain activity, a published case). Entries without a public source are listed as declared.
  • A mandate. Write it against the Open Mandate schema and send the JSON document, or open a pull request in the site repository. The Index assigns the ID and lists it as declared.

Agents can do this too: curl -s https://agenticfinancereport.com/skill.md describes every endpoint.

Methodology, v0.1

  • Layers follow the report's stack: regulated foundation, execution, inference, orchestration, identity, runtime protection, and agents themselves.
  • Status is in production when a public source shows clients or live activity; standards live when the standard exists and deployment is in progress; forward-looking when it is announced or designed but not running; declared when the only source is the operator. The source is named in every entry.
  • Figures supplied by an operator and not independently verified are labelled as such wherever they appear.
  • Disputes. An operator or a third party can dispute an entry by email with a source. The dispute, the evidence and the outcome are logged in the corrections below.
  • What the Index is not. Not a rating, not an endorsement, not an attestation, not investment advice. Attestation of enforcement is a separate service that does not exist yet and will not be sold to the party being attested.
